# Production screenshots

The files under `product-flow/` are deterministic crops and WebP exports of
the hosted SlopProof product. They are not captures of `/demo`, archived brand
concepts, or generated interface artwork.

- `contributor-proof.webp`: current-sha entry point with the SHA blurred.
- `practice.webp`: patch map and separate understanding coach.
- `one-take.webp`: mobile live question with a concrete patch reference.
- `privacy-check.webp`: camera and privacy preflight; the complete camera
  viewport is blurred.
- `review-evidence.webp`: protected maintainer review; the evidence video is
  blurred, and the transcript and full SHA are outside the crop.

The source captures remain outside the repository. Public derivatives contain
no capability, usable QR code, private transcript, or readable face/room view.
The landing publisher copies these exact bytes to `landing/product-flow/`.

The review capture intentionally shows `Needs a look`. It documents the
fail-closed path rather than pretending every automated judgment is a pass.
