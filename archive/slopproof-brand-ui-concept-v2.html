Team Humanity

Tools can generate code. They cannot take responsibility.

SlopProof does not punish assistance. It protects the human act that comes after it: understanding the change, naming its risks and standing behind the merge.

No surveillance. No purity test. No invented AI score.Fast code still deserves a slow thought.

The accountability chain

A merge stays connected to an act of understanding.

The mechanism is short enough for real projects and concrete enough to create useful friction.

01 / OPEN

A pull request arrives.

SlopProof prepares a required check and reads only the context needed to ask a fair question.

02 / RESPOND

The author starts when ready.

A timed, patch-specific prompt appears. The repository decides which response modes are accepted.

03 / CLARIFY

One follow-up tests the explanation.

The question points to a claim, omission or edge case in the first response.

04 / DECIDE

A maintainer remains accountable.

Assistance can structure the evidence. Only the maintainer passes or requests another attempt.

MVP+2 · multimodal by policy

Different ways to explain. The same standard of understanding.

Maintainers enable the modes that fit their community. Contributors choose only from those options. No mode is treated as a measure of human worth.

LIVE ARTIFACTPRIVATE · SHORT RETENTION

Sketch the behavior. Show the nonce. Explain the edge case.

SlopProof normalizes the image, strips metadata and extracts text for review. Uncertain handwriting goes to the maintainer with the original still visible.

Choose photo or use camera
LIVE TEXTNO TYPING BIOMETRICS

Write the explanation while the patch context is live.

The server enforces the time window and a hard size limit. SlopProof does not use typing rhythm, pauses or key patterns as identity signals.

AUDIOTRANSCRIBED FOR REVIEW

Talk through the change without turning on a camera.

The browser chooses a supported format, shows a preview and asks before upload. Transcription confidence is never treated as understanding confidence.

VIDEONO FACE REQUIRED

Point at a diagram, terminal or handwritten explanation.

Video is an optional response format, never a face check. The worker creates a small review copy and extracts audio for transcription.

Live challenge · in practice

Understanding can be shown, spoken, written or demonstrated.

These are response formats, not identity tests. The repository chooses what it accepts; the contributor chooses how to explain within that policy.

The contributor sees the task and retention rules. The maintainer sees evidence, uncertainty and the final decision. Repository policy stays explicit.

Trust boundary

Public accountability. Private evidence.

The check communicates that responsibility was taken. It does not publish the contributor’s response or pretend to establish identity.

What the public check says

  • Challenge status for the current revision
  • Completion timestamp
  • Maintainer decision
  • No media, transcript or private reasoning

What stays protected

  • Paper, text, audio and video responses
  • OCR and transcript derivatives
  • Follow-up answers and review reasons
  • Automatic deletion on a short retention clock
